The ST-LAN Team at FENS Forum 2026 in Barcelona

marco uderzo July 14, 2026
fens posters ejn

We are happy to announce that our lab attended FENS Forum 2026 in Barcelona, the largest neuroscience conference in Europe.

Here are the main highlights:

Marco and Renato presented two posters showcasing their PhD project results

Marco’s artwork was chosen as the winning image of the FENS-EJN Image Competition and was publicly displayed at FENS!

“My sincere thanks to the European Journal of Neuroscience for selecting my artwork and for recognizing that science and art don’t need to be separate paths in life.” – Marco

Dr. Zayna Chaker is part of the 2026 cohort of FENS-Kavli Scholars!

The Federation of European Neuroscience Societies and The Kavli Foundation have announced the 2026 cohort of FENS-Kavli Scholars, naming 15 early- to mid-career neuroscientists whose national origins span 12 countries across Europe, the Americas, and Asia. FENS-Kavli Scholars always are recognized as the next generation of leaders in European neuroscience. Dr. Zayna Chaker is among these. Congratulations to her!
