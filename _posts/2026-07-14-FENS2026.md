## Dr. Zayna Chaker is part of the 2026 cohort of FENS-Kavli Scholars!

The Federation of European Neuroscience Societies and The Kavli Foundation have announced the 2026 cohort of FENS-Kavli Scholars, naming 15 early- to mid-career neuroscientists whose national origins span 12 countries across Europe, the Americas, and Asia. FENS-Kavli Scholars always are recognized as the next generation of leaders in European neuroscience.
Dr. Zayna Chaker is among these. Congratulations to her!
