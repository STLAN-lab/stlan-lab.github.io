The ST-LAN team took part in the launch event of the Zebra Lyon network, a collaborative initiative bringing together zebrafish research teams in Lyon.

On this occasion, Léa and Marilyne presented the research activities conducted by the ST-LAN and Ruggiero teams using the zebrafish model.

We are pleased to join this network and look forward to fostering scientific exchanges and new collaborations!
