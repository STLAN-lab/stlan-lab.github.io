Suzy started her career at INRAE in 1992 as a technician and evolve to the status of research engineer. She is mainly interested in developing protocols and tools necessary to answer precise scientific questions, with a long-lasting experience in molecular and cellular biology. This last decade, she developed a solid expertise in genome editing and works part time with a transgenic core facility for the generation of mouse models using CRISPR/Cas9 technology.
